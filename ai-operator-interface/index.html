CASE STUDY

From requirements to operator decision.

This project is intentionally small: the focus is not model sophistication, but how AI output is translated into a usable, accountable GUI workflow.

01

GUI requirements

Surface system health, anomalies, confidence and recommended action without hiding operator control.

02

Human factors

Use severity, plain-language status and explicit actions to reduce ambiguity under time pressure.

03

AI interaction

Represent a simple anomaly-detection model through confidence, explanation and recommendation states.

04

Prototype logic

Model normal, warning and critical states with conditional transitions, modal review and persistent audit feedback.
